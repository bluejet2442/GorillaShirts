Manual checks still needed
1. Start Gorilla Tag and check the stand and installed packs.
2. Wear head, body, arm, and hand items; check wobble physics and custom colours.
3. Change the name-tag offset and restart to check saved preferences.
4. Join a room with another mod user, change outfits, then leave and rejoin.
5. Check the BepInEx log for errors and verify behaviour without internet access.

Install only after backing up the existing DLL outside BepInEx/plugins.
If you test without GorillaLibrary, remember that other mods may still need it.
To roll back, close the game and restore your backed-up DLL.
